DESIGN DIRECTION — 「深色沉浸考试风」: a dark, focus-first professional aesthetic. Deep charcoal page background (not pure black), panels lifted one step above the surface, near-white body text, one high-chroma accent for primary actions and a clearly separate warning hue reserved for the anti-cheat banner and the flagged state. On 答题页 the chrome recedes and the question text becomes the brightest thing on screen; on 首页 and 我的 the density stays readable. Use luminance and contrast, not decoration, to build hierarchy. No neon glow, no glassmorphism, no gradient meshes, no stock photography. Feels like a serious pro-grade study tool you can stare at at 11pm.
